## 🛠️ 2. Dokumentasi Khusus per Alat (Modular Tools Reference)

Seluruh alat dan fitur telah dipecah ke dalam berkas modular mandiri di folder `docs/tools/`:

1. [Pen / Pulpen Tinta](file:///home/hiawsaputro/APP/awnotes/docs/tools/01-pen.md) - Goresan solid dengan sensitivitas tekanan.
2. [Pensil (Grade 2H–6B)](file:///home/hiawsaputro/APP/awnotes/docs/tools/02-pencil.md) - Sistem tingkat kekerasan & kepekatan pensil standar.
3. [Stabilo / Highlighter](file:///home/hiawsaputro/APP/awnotes/docs/tools/03-highlighter.md) - Ujung miring dengan efek *multiply blend* transparan.
4. [Penghapus / Eraser](file:///home/hiawsaputro/APP/awnotes/docs/tools/04-eraser.md) - Mode *Stroke Eraser* (garis utuh) & *Pixel Eraser* (area).
5. [Objek Dasar & Hold to Snap](file:///home/hiawsaputro/APP/awnotes/docs/tools/05-shapes.md) - Geometri presisi & tahan stylus untuk merapikan garis.
6. [Lasso Tool](file:///home/hiawsaputro/APP/awnotes/docs/tools/06-lasso.md) - Seleksi bebas untuk menggeser, memperbesar, dan mewarnai ulang.
7. [Pad Penulisan Mudah (Easy Writing Pad)](file:///home/hiawsaputro/APP/awnotes/docs/tools/07-easy-writing-pad.md) - Kotak zoom bawah & auto-advance untuk layar kecil.
8. [Mode Teks & Checklist To-Do](file:///home/hiawsaputro/APP/awnotes/docs/tools/08-text-and-checklist.md) - Pengetikan hybrid dan daftar tugas centang.
9. [Gambar & Kunci Objek (Lock Image)](file:///home/hiawsaputro/APP/awnotes/docs/tools/09-images-and-media.md) - Paste clipboard `Ctrl+V` dan gembok gambar.
10. [Anotasi PDF & Ekspor Dokumen](file:///home/hiawsaputro/APP/awnotes/docs/tools/10-pdf-annotation.md) - Render PDF multi-lembar & ekspor PDF/PNG/`.awn`.
11. [Sinkronisasi Audio (Audio Sync)](file:///home/hiawsaputro/APP/awnotes/docs/tools/11-audio-sync.md) - Rekaman suara sinkron goresan tinta.
